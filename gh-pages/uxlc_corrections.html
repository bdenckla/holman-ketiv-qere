Daniel Holman's suggestions for the UXLC

Daniel Holman has been sending Chris Kimball and Ben Denckla suggested changes to the UXLC, a book at a time, and this page collects them. Most are places where he reads the Leningrad Codex images differently from the text as it stands; the rest ask the UXLC for a note on a reading Holman and the UXLC agree about.

Each card is one case, and its labelled lines are Holman's, quoted from the email as he wrote them, including his spellings of the accent names. Two things on a card are not: the manuscript location, which is estimated here rather than quoted; and one word of his Lev 25:20 case, which had the UXLC's note letter c sitting inside it. That letter is the note's name rather than part of the word, so it and the invisible marks his mail client wrapped it in are dropped here.

The manuscript location on a card is not Holman's. He names the scan file he worked from and puts the atom in a band of a column of it, as Col. 2 middle; the file name says nothing a reader can use, so the card reports an estimated column and line instead, worked out from the page breaks the UXLC's Leningrad Codex index records and the count of atoms between them. It agrees with his column on 19 of the 20 cases, and the Leviticus 7:25 card gives his reading beside the estimate. The band is left out of that comparison, top, middle and bottom being a gloss that a line number supersedes. The folio link under the line is decoded from the page ordinal Holman's citation begins with, so the folio number is not his either.

A word in square brackets is Ben Denckla's, put there afterwards in place of the word the message has: on the subject of the message of 07 August 2026, Joshua is shown as [Judges]; on the Note line of Judges 11:24, Tet is shown as [Tav]. The one other bracketed phrase on the page is not a correction of that kind: [address removed] stands wherever a message gave an email address.

A card also links whatever the UXLC's editor has entered for that word in 2026.10.19 - Changes, the changes proposed for UXLC 2.6. Of the 20 cases, 14 have such a change item and 6 have none. The Joshua 20:4 case has two, one per word of a compound whose two marks the case asks to exchange. A change item is the editor's wording rather than Holman's, and the Judges 11:24 one is older than the message quoted beside it: Ben Denckla entered it in April, on the same word and about the same indistinct mark.

Nothing here is a decision. The suggestions are Chris Kimball's to accept or decline for the UXLC.

#4 Exodus 38:12, atom 6 UXLC MwD Change 2026.08.05-5 Exodus
Word / Verse
Ex 38:12.6 UXLC (עַמּוּדֵיהֶ֥ם)
Manuscript location
Column 1, line 4 or thereabouts. folio 055A
Suggestion
Add note OR replace Mereka with Munach (עַמּוּדֵיהֶ֣ם).
Critical Note
Going by appearance, this mark does not look like a Mereka due to its slant. Dotan has a Munach. This word is in the domain of Zaqef Qatan, and the servant of Zaqef Qatan is always Munach. Price noted BHS and BHK erroneously have Mereka, which is possibly a defect in L (Page 65, Syntax of Masoretic Accents).

Holman's case 4 in Proposed Corrections and Notes for UXLC (Exodus 7, 11, 20 & 38), 06 August 2026.

#5 Leviticus 7:25, atom 6 UXLC MwD Change 2026.08.05-7 Leviticus

Lev 7:25.6 UXLC (הַ֨בְּהֵמָ֔ה)
Manuscript location
Column 3, line 2 or thereabouts. Holman gives Col. 2 bottom. folio 060A
Suggestion
Make a note about accent positioning.
Critical Note
Some may read this accent as a Pashta. Medieval scribes did not rely on physical positioning to differentiate a Pashta from an Azla. A note should be included to prevent graphic ambiguity for less fluent readers.

#8 Leviticus 25:20, atom 4 UXLC MwD Change 2026.08.05-10 Leviticus
Word / Verse
Lev 25:20.4 UXLC (מַה־נֹּאכַ֤֖ל)
Manuscript location
Column 2, line 14 or thereabouts. folio 071B
Suggestion
Remove Mahpakh and revise note (מַה־נֹּאכַ֖ל).
Critical Note
This mark is a Masorah Circle for the word below it. There is no angle to the mark for a Mahpakh and the shape matches that of other Masorah Circles. The Masorah Circle intersected part of the Patach as noted in the images.

#9 Deuteronomy 33:28, atom 13 UXLC MwD Change 2026.08.05-11 Deuteronomy
Word/Verse
Deut 33:28.13 UXLC (יַֽעַרְפוּ / יַ֥עַרְפוּ)
Manuscript location
Column 3, line 23 or thereabouts. folio 119B
Suggestion
Change Meteg to Mercha OR include an apparatus note detailing the manuscript variants.
Critical Note
Dotan features a Mercha. In Daat Miqra, Breuer reads both Aleppo (A) and Leningrad (L) as having a Mercha. Conversely, Sassoon 1053 and Jacob ben Chayyim print a Meteg, but crucially include a Maqqef to bind the word to the subsequent Silluq. If the Meteg tradition is maintained, an editorial note should indicate that alternate manuscripts require a Maqqef; an independent word cannot sit accentless directly preceding a Silluq without violating Tiberian accentuation syntax.

Holman's case 1 in Proposed Correction and Note for UXLC (Deuteronomy 33), 07 August 2026.

#13 Joshua 20:4, atom 12 UXLC MwD Change 2026.08.05-15 Change 2026.08.05-16 Joshua
Current UXLC
זִקְנֵ֥י־הָעִֽיר (with notes c and m)
Suggested Text
זִקְנֵֽי־הָעִ֥יר
Manuscript location
Column 2, line 9 or thereabouts. folio 132B
Suggestion
Remove notes c and m; reverse the positions of the Mereka and Meteg.
Note
In Daat Miqra, Breuer notes Aleppo (A) and L agree on this reading, placing a Meteg under Nun and a Mereka under Ayin. Dotan shares this reading in BHL. Notes c and m represent subjective interpretations rather than absolute readings of L. Between the two marks in L, the mark under Ayin slants sharply, looking much more like a Mereka.

Holman's case 4 in Suggested UXLC Corrections for Joshua (4 Cases), 07 August 2026.

וַיַּעַזְבוּ שָׁ֖ם
Manuscript location
Column 3, line 27 or thereabouts. folio 171A
Suggestion
Remove Maqqef.
Note
Several letters touch here, mimicking a Maqqef, particularly between the Zayin-Beit and Shin-Final Mem. These look like ink trails left as the quill exited the parchment. However, a distinct, empty space separates the two words, confirming no Maqqef exists.
