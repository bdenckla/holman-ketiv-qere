Daniel Holman's suggested UXLC corrections

Daniel Holman has been sending Chris Kimball and Ben Denckla suggested corrections to the UXLC, a book at a time, and this page collects them. Most are places where he reads the Leningrad Codex images differently from the text as it stands; the rest ask the UXLC for a note on a reading Holman and the UXLC agree about, and the "What Holman asks for" column below counts each kind.

Each card is one case, and its labelled lines are Holman's, quoted from the email exactly as he wrote them, including his spellings of the accent names. One thing on a card is added rather than quoted: on the line giving the manuscript image, the folio link and the Internet Archive link after Holman's citation are decoded here from the page ordinal that citation begins with, so the folio number is not Holman's either.

Nothing here is a decision. The suggestions are Chris Kimball's to accept or decline for the UXLC.

#4 Exodus 38:12, atom 6 UXLC MwD MAM-ws mgketer Exodus Change the text or add a note
Word / Verse
Ex 38:12.6 UXLC (עַמּוּדֵיהֶ֥ם)
Manuscript Image
109_Exo_38.11b-39.3a / Col. 1 top folio 055A Internet Archive
Suggested Correction
Add note OR replace Mereka with Munach (עַמּוּדֵיהֶ֣ם).
Critical Note
Going by appearance, this mark does not look like a Mereka due to its slant. Dotan has a Munach. This word is in the domain of Zaqef Qatan, and the servant of Zaqef Qatan is always Munach. Price noted BHS and BHK erroneously have Mereka, which is possibly a defect in L (Page 65, Syntax of Masoretic Accents).

#9 Deuteronomy 33:28, atom 13 UXLC MwD MAM-ws mgketer Deuteronomy Change the text or add a note
Word/Verse
Deut 33:28.13 UXLC (יַֽעַרְפוּ / יַ֥עַרְפוּ)
Manuscript Image
238_Deu_32.52b-33.29 / Col. 3 bottom folio 119B Internet Archive
Suggested Correction
Change Meteg to Mercha OR include an apparatus note detailing the manuscript variants.
Critical Note
Dotan features a Mercha. In Daat Miqra, Breuer reads both Aleppo (A) and Leningrad (L) as having a Mercha. Conversely, Sassoon 1053 and Jacob ben Chayyim print a Meteg, but crucially include a Maqqef to bind the word to the subsequent Silluq. If the Meteg tradition is maintained, an editorial note should indicate that alternate manuscripts require a Maqqef; an independent word cannot sit accentless directly preceding a Silluq without violating Tiberian accentuation syntax.

Holman's case 1 in Proposed Correction and Note for UXLC (Deuteronomy 33), 07 August 2026.

#13 Joshua 20:4, atom 12 UXLC MwD MAM-ws mgketer Joshua Change the text
Current UXLC
זִקְנֵ֥י־הָעִֽיר (with notes c and m)
Image Location
264_Jos_19.42b-21.5a (Col. 2 middle) folio 132B Internet Archive
Suggested Correction
Remove notes c and m; reverse the positions of the Mereka and Meteg.
Corrected Text
זִקְנֵֽי־הָעִ֥יר
Note
In Daat Miqra, Breuer notes Aleppo (A) and L agree on this reading, placing a Meteg under Nun and a Mereka under Ayin. Dotan shares this reading in BHL. Notes c and m represent subjective interpretations rather than absolute readings of L. Between the two marks in L, the mark under Ayin slants sharply, looking much more like a Mereka.

Suggested Correction
Remove Maqqef.
Corrected Text
